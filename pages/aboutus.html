About Us

Our Story

Treeprint was founded in 2026 with a simple belief: small actions can create lasting change. While trees are being planted in communities around the world, there was no easy way to see the bigger picture or understand where more trees were needed.

Treeprint was created to bring this information together in one accessible platform. By combining mapping technology, community participation and environmental data, Treeprint makes it easier for people to discover planting projects, record their contributions and support reforestation.

Today, Treeprint connects volunteers, organisations and donors who share a common goal: creating healthier, greener communities for future generations.

Our Mission

Empowering communities to make a difference.
Our mission is to empower communities through technology by tracking tree-planting activities, identifying areas that need more trees and encouraging people to take an active role in restoring the environment.

Our Vision

A greener planet, one community at a time.
Our vision is to become a trusted global platform for tree-planting awareness and environmental action, helping communities work together to create a healthier and more sustainable planet.
